Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026.

This policy covers this website and VISION, the personal publishing tool that Mauricio Alonso Moya Tapia runs for his own social media accounts (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook).

Who uses VISION

VISION is used only by its owner, to post his own videos to his own accounts. It is not offered to the public, and no one else signs in to it.

What data it uses

  • Sign-in tokens for the owner's own accounts, kept only in the owner's computer keychain.
  • The owner's own videos, captions and post settings, to publish them.
  • Public view, like and comment counts for the owner's own posts, to learn what works.

What it does not do

  • It does not collect data about other people.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.
  • It does not use the data for advertising.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove VISION's access at any time from each platform's account settings, which deletes the stored token's use. Locally stored tokens are deleted when the account is removed from VISION.

This website

This website does not use cookies or tracking and does not collect personal data.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/maumoya/maumoya.github.io.
