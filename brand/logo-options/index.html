Six directions for the Mauricio Moya mark. Each is shown on light and dark, at app-icon, favicon (32 and 16 px), browser tab and 44 px header size, plus the full lockup. The four colours stand for the four areas:

  • Finance
  • Technology
  • Compliance
  • Education

One M, four strokes: each stroke is one area. Reads at 16 px as a solid tile.
