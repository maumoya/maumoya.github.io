The algorithm

Total = measured (35) + rubric (60) + panel (5). Every rubric score s from 1 to 10 counts as (s − 1) / 9 of its weight. Measured scores come from headless Chrome renders at 128 px and 16 px on ivory.

16 px fidelity: Pearson correlation between the 16 px render (upscaled) and the 128 px render; full points at r ≥ 0.98, zero at 0.80. Contrast: WCAG ratio between the 2nd and 98th luminance percentile of the mark's centre; full points at 7:1. One colour: number of hue families a mark needs (a gold gradient counts as one). Simplicity: SVG elements + path commands / 2 + letters; fewer means easier to engrave, stitch or emboss. Circle form: the owner's rule that circles work better.

Personality target

From the 12-archetype brand model: Ruler (authority, trust: finance and compliance) + Sage (wisdom: education) + a touch of Creator (technology). The rubric's Personality fit, Professional and Luxury columns score against this target: private bank, Swiss watch, university seal. Playful, startup-coloured marks (A1 to A3) score low here by design.

Sources and tests

Criteria were taken from published agent skills for brand and logo work, read only, nothing installed: a brand-identity skill (16 px legibility, one-colour use, silhouette, application stress test), a logo-design skill (SVG audit, complexity, detail size, the 96 to 16 px size ladder, squint and reversed tests, 3:1 minimum contrast) and a brand-identity skill with the 12 archetypes. The persona simulation (MiroFish swarm, six audience personas) ran twice and failed both times without a ranking, so the panel scores 0 for every option and the live poll replaces it with real votes.
